Thursday, October 1, 2026

The King of Dust

✎Writing
Now that I have received my gifts, I must unwrap them, I unravel and they are gifts no more, but things to be lost. King Midas had a touch which poisoned every thought with a lick of genius. What if he had a touch of dirt? It would not be sufficient for him to only turn his food into soot, or his clothes, his child’s favorite toy to dust. He may only touch everything, building up until he may construct himself a throne in his empire of soil, only then can he have a title such as “king”. With everything made, yet not quite in his image, he may touch, and be gone by morning
